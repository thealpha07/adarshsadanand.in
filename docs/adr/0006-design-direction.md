# Design direction B: midnight front door, calm rooms

Status: accepted, 11 Oct 2026

## Context

The owner wanted an expressive homepage in a cool tone, with comfortable reading.

## Decision

Expressive homepage (huge Archivo type, cut-out portrait, hairline grid); subpages share the midnight band but read in Atkinson Hyperlegible at 19px within 68ch.

## Alternatives not chosen

- **Direction A, warm editorial**: Calm, but not the expressive feel that was asked for.
- **Everything expressive**: Long text in 76px uppercase is tiring to read.

## Consequences

The Azathoth design system (v2) is the source of truth for tokens.
